# 🌟 Little Champions: Play & Learn (छोटे उस्ताद - खेल खेल में सीखें)

A vibrant, kid-friendly, sound-rich educational game designed for **Mobile Phones, Tablets, and Desktop Computers**!

---

## 📱 How to Play on Your Phone & Tablet

You can play this game directly on your smartphone (Android, iPhone, iPad) with rich animations, touch controls, and crystal-clear sound!

### Method 1: One-Click Phone Launcher
1. Double click **`run_phone_server.bat`** (or choose option `[1]` in **`run_game.bat`**).
2. A terminal window opens showing:
   - Your local Wi-Fi link (e.g. `http://192.168.1.92:8000`).
   - A scannable **QR Code** right on your computer screen.
3. **Open your Phone's Camera** or Chrome / Safari browser, and either scan the QR code or type `http://192.168.1.92:8000`.
4. *(Optional PWA)*: Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** on your phone browser to install it like a real native app with full-screen experience!

## 🎮 Features Implemented (All 10 Requirements)

### 1. 📖 Traditional Multiplication Tables (1 to 20)
- Traditional rhythmic recitations in **English**:
  - *"Two ones are two"*
  - *"Two twos are four"*
  - *"Two threes are six"*
- Traditional rhythmic recitations in **Hindi**:
  - *"दो एकम दो"*
  - *"दो दूनी चार"*
  - *"दो तिया छह"*
- Row-by-row audio reciter with visual row highlighting.
- Memory tricks for every table from 1 to 20.
- Interactive **Table Quiz Game** to test multiplication speed!

### 2. 🎮 Interactive Educational Games
- Balloon pop games for English, Hindi, and Mathematics.
- Division Safari with sharing stories and animated treats (cupcakes, stars, apples).
- Instant celebratory confetti, victory trumpet fanfares, and stars rewards.

### 3. 🗣️ Intelligent Voice Options & Auto Gender Detection
- **Male (Mentor)** & **Female (Teacher)** voice options.
- **Smart Automatic Detection by Name**:
  - Girls' names (e.g. *Preeti, Ananya, Priya, Pooja, Riya, Neha*) ➔ Warm **Female Voice**.
  - Boys' names (e.g. *Harsh, Aarav, Rohan, Aryan, Kabir, Rahul*) ➔ Cheerful **Male Voice**.
- Instant voice toggle pill right in the top header bar and in the Profile dialog.

### 4. 🎓 Class-Wise Learning (Nursery to Class 5)
- Filtering and recommended learning modules based on the student's exact school grade:
  - 🐣 **Nursery to Class 1**
  - 🎓 **Class 1 to Class 5**
- Quick one-click filter tabs on the home screen.

### 5. 📄 Downloadable & Printable Worksheets Center
- Practice worksheets ready to print or save as PDF via browser print (`Ctrl+P` / Print button).
- Includes worksheets for **Hindi**, **English**, and **Mathematics** (with date, score, and student name header).

### 6. 🐣 Nursery to Class 1
- **English Alphabet (A-Z)**: Giant flashcards, phonics, and Floating Balloon Pop arcade.
- **हिन्दी वर्णमाला**: १३ स्वर (अ से अः) व ३६ व्यंजन (क से ज्ञ) शुद्ध ध्वनि उच्चारण सहित।
- **1 to 100 Counting**: Tap-to-count animated animals/fruits with English & Hindi names.
- **Tens to 1000**: Values like 10, 20, 30... 100, 200, 300... 1000.
- **Colours & Shapes Recognition**: Interactive color palette and geometric shapes with fun questions.
- **Rhymes & Animated Poems Stage**: Twinkle Twinkle, Titli Udi, Chanda Mama, and Machhli Jal Ki Rani with singing narration!

### 7. 🎓 Class 1 to Class 5
- **Open Dictation Studio**: Listen to spoken words, practice difficult spellings, get phonetic hints and example sentences.
- **Brain Puzzles & Riddles**: Teasers and riddles that improve critical thinking.
- **General Knowledge (GK)**: National symbols, solar system, and science trivia.

### 8. ➕ Advanced Mathematics
- **Addition & Subtraction** quizzes.
- **Division Safari** (sharing puzzles).
- **Multiplication Detective** (missing factors).
- **Find the Value of X** (algebra equations like $x + 6 = 15$, $3x = 21$).
- **Fractions & Decimals** (visual pizza slices and decimal conversions).
- **LCM & HCF** factor clouds and step-by-step multiple rows.

### 9. 📖 Creative Storytelling & Story Creator Studio
- Illustrated bilingual stories (The Clever Crow / चतुर कौआ, The Lion & Mouse / शेर और चूहा) with comprehension quizzes.
- **"Create Your Own Story" Builder**: Kids choose their hero, magical setting, secret item, and ending to build and hear their own unique adventure story!

### 10. 🌈 Colorful, Vibrant & Child-Friendly UI
- Bright pastel gradients, bubbly cards, bouncy hover effects, shiny badges, and animated mascots.
- Fully responsive on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop monitors.
